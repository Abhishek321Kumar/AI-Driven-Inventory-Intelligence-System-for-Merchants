## Platform
web

## Users
Retail and wholesale store merchants, boutique fashion store owners (like Rajesh Sharma of Sharma Apparels), and small-to-medium retail business operators managing inventory and working capital.

## Product Purpose
Mercura AI ("Smarter Inventory. Stronger Tomorrow.") transforms dormant inventory into liquid working capital. It gives merchants unambiguous mathematical guidance on what to reorder, what to stop buying, which slow-moving SKUs to discount, and where their next ₹10,000 of working capital will yield the highest return.

## Positioning
An AI-driven, human-authorized Working Capital & POS Ingestion Engine. Unlike passive ERPs or generic accounting dashboards, Mercura AI enforces strict merchant authority (zero auto-buying risk) while continuously running ML telemetry (94.2% ML forecast precision) to identify stagnant stock and release locked cash.

## Operating Context
Fast-paced retail counter environments, desktop/tablet POS terminals, end-of-day sales reconciliation, multi-channel invoice ingestion (CSV, XLSX, OCR receipt/invoice image scanning, POS sync), and strategic weekly inventory reordering.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Multi-format ingestion engine: Excel (.xlsx, .xls), CSV, Image OCR receipt scanning, adaptive manual SKU entry.
- 6 interconnected intelligence loops: Multi-Channel POS Sync, Dead Capital Diagnosis, Intelligent Liquidation Engine, Reorder Guardrail Engine, Working Capital Allocation, Continuous Margin Telemetry.
- Strict Merchant Control: 100% human authorization required before executing reorders or vendor payments.
- Real-time reactive data store with LocalStorage persistence and Demo Data reset capability.
- Multi-view responsive workspace: Home (Vision), Dashboard, Inventory, Sales & Demand, Stock Alerts, AI Recommendations, Cash Flow, Settings.

## Product Principles
1. Total Merchant Authority: Never execute purchase orders automatically without explicit merchant sign-off.
2. Capital Clarity: Always translate abstract metrics into tangible rupee values (cash recovered, money locked, ROI).
3. Actionable Telemetry: Every risk diagnosis must include a ready-to-execute suggested merchant action.
4. Institutional Grade: FinTech-level precision with high aesthetic polish and zero visual slop.
